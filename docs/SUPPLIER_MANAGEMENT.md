# Tulsi Mart – Supplier & Vendor Management

## 1. Overview
The Supplier Management module coordinates procurement, purchase records, vendor balances, and payment disbursement.

---

## 2. Dashboard Metrics
- **Total Suppliers**: Active vendor count.
- **Pending Orders**: Orders awaiting goods delivery.
- **Payable Balance**: Total pending balance owed to suppliers.
- **Total Purchases**: Cumulative purchase volume.

---

## 3. Supplier Profiles & Quick Communication
Each supplier record contains:
- Contact Person Name
- Company / Firm Name
- Phone number (with one-tap direct calling)
- Email address
- GSTIN
- Delivery & billing address

---

## 4. Supplier Ledger & Payments
- Receiving goods credits the supplier's balance in `supplier_ledger`.
- Tapping "Pay Supplier" allows recording payment via Bank Transfer (NEFT/RTGS), UPI, Cash, or Cheque, updating the payable balance.
- Tapping "Statement" generates an exportable statement of recent orders and payments for reconciliation.
